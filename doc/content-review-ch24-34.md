# Content review: chapters 24–34

Review date: October 18, 2026.

**Correction status:** All 57 numbered findings and the 13 additional items below have been addressed in the textbook. The revisions keep the explanations at an introductory level and reconcile related summaries, glossary entries, captions, and diagrams. End-of-section exercises, problems, and their solutions remain outside this revision's scope.

The original review is retained below as a record of the issues and supporting sources. Its descriptions and line numbers refer to the text before correction.

**Validation:** The site build and checks for math delimiters, YAML, document structure, figures, and internal cross-references passed. All 49 new or changed math expressions also rendered with MathJax without errors. `git diff --check` passed.

This review focused on scientific explanations, definitions, main-text equations, captions, internal consistency, and dated factual claims. Worked-example calculations were not systematically recalculated during the review, and this was not an exhaustive typesetting or spelling audit.

The main teaching sequence is useful: electromagnetic waves → optics → relativity → quantum physics → atomic and nuclear physics → particle physics and cosmology. The review identified consequential errors within that sequence. Chapters 30–34 required the most substantive revision, including chapter 32's medical-physics terminology.

**Priority:** High means the passage teaches an incorrect physical rule, gives consequential incorrect data, or substantially misstates the evidence. Medium means an explanation needs qualification, an application is misdescribed, or historical/current information needs correction. Locations below refer to the reviewed Markdown line numbers.

## Chapter 24 — Electromagnetic waves

1. **High — Microwave heating is incorrectly presented as molecular resonance.** [Energy in Electromagnetic Waves, line 19](../contents/ch24EnergyInElectromagneticWaves.md#L19) gives microwaves at water's resonant frequency as its example of resonance-enhanced absorption. Microwave ovens heat liquid water mainly through dielectric loss as dipoles respond to an alternating field; 2.45 GHz is not a special molecular resonance of liquid water. This also conflicts with the better dielectric-heating explanation in [The Electromagnetic Spectrum, line 393](../contents/ch24TheElectromagneticSpectrum.md#L393). Replace the resonance example and connect the two explanations. [Dielectric-relaxation teaching material](https://www.lehigh.edu/IMI/teched/JapanWS/WS11AB_Lanagan.pdf).

2. **High — Jaundice phototherapy is assigned to the wrong spectral band.** [The Electromagnetic Spectrum, line 708](../contents/ch24TheElectromagneticSpectrum.md#L708) says UV treats infantile jaundice. The same error recurs in [chapter 29, line 217](../contents/ch29PhotonEnergiesAndTheElectromagneticSpectrum.md#L217). Neonatal bilirubin phototherapy uses visible blue/blue-green light, approximately 460–490 nm. Correct both passages together. [American Academy of Pediatrics technical report](https://publications.aap.org/pediatrics/article/154/3/e2024068026/199038/Phototherapy-to-Prevent-Severe-Neonatal?autologincheck=redirected).

3. **High — The vitamin-D discussion turns an association into a causal claim and recommends unprotected exposure.** [The Electromagnetic Spectrum, line 698](../contents/ch24TheElectromagneticSpectrum.md#L698) suggests vitamin-D deficiency causes several cancers and offers ten minutes of daily exposure without sunscreen. Keep the physical explanation of UVB-driven synthesis, but remove the general exposure prescription and unsupported cancer-prevention implication. Exposure requirements vary, and dermatological guidance recommends dietary sources rather than deliberate UV exposure. [American Academy of Dermatology guidance](https://www.aad.org/media/stats-vitamin-d).

4. **High — Food-irradiation safety is framed as an unresolved long-term hazard.** [The Electromagnetic Spectrum, line 833](../contents/ch24TheElectromagneticSpectrum.md#L833) says long-term hazards are unknown. Chapter 32 repeats and expands this framing. Distinguish public controversy from the evidence supporting approved irradiation processes. The FDA reports that it has evaluated their safety and found them safe under approved conditions. [FDA explanation](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/food-irradiation-what-you-need-know).

5. **Medium — The UV subdivisions and atmospheric absorption account need correction.** [The Electromagnetic Spectrum, line 607](../contents/ch24TheElectromagneticSpectrum.md#L607) gives UVC as only 220–290 nm and attributes the absorption of all UVC to ozone. Use a named convention consistently: WHO uses UVA 315–400 nm, UVB 280–315 nm, and UVC 100–280 nm. Oxygen and other atmospheric constituents also contribute to absorption. A 320-nm UVA/UVB boundary exists in some conventions, but the current UVC range is incomplete. [WHO spectral definitions](https://www.who.int/health-topics/ultraviolet-radiation).

6. **Medium — Characteristic X-ray production is confused with recapture of a free electron.** [The Electromagnetic Spectrum, line 732](../contents/ch24TheElectromagneticSpectrum.md#L732) and the following paragraph describe an ionized atom capturing an electron that falls into the vacant inner orbit. For the standard X-ray-tube explanation, describe an existing higher-shell electron filling an inner-shell vacancy. Chapter 30 already gives this account in [X-Rays, line 51](../contents/ch30XRays.md#L51). Reconcile the earlier text and caption with that explanation.

## Chapter 25 — Geometric optics

7. **High — Thinness is incorrectly defined as eliminating dispersion and aberrations.** [Image Formation by Lenses, line 197](../contents/ch25ImageFormationByLenses.md#L197) and its definition box at line 212 say a thin lens does not allow those properties. A thin lens has thickness small enough to neglect in the geometric model. It can still have chromatic and spherical aberrations. State separately the thin-lens and paraxial/ideal-lens assumptions. The present definition also conflicts with chapter 26's treatment of real lenses.

8. **High — Two perpendicular mirrors are claimed to retroreflect any incident ray.** [Total Internal Reflection, line 212](../contents/ch25TotalInternalReflection.md#L212) makes this claim independent of incidence direction. Two mirrors reverse the components perpendicular to their common edge but preserve the component along it. The claim works for rays in the illustrated cross-sectional plane; unrestricted three-dimensional retroreflection requires three mutually perpendicular reflecting surfaces. Add the planar restriction and introduce the three-face corner cube for the practical examples.

9. **Medium — The speed of light is called an SI unit.** [The Law of Refraction, line 38](../contents/ch25TheLawOfRefraction.md#L38) calls it one of four fundamental SI units. It is a defining physical constant, not a unit; the SI has seven base units. State that the exact value of c helps define the metre.

## Chapter 26 — Vision and optical instruments

10. **High — Rods are assigned exclusive responsibility for peripheral vision.** [Color and Color Vision, line 33](../contents/ch26ColorAndColorVision.md#L33) says rods are solely responsible for peripheral vision. The peripheral retina contains cones as well as rods; their relative contributions depend on illumination. Describe rods as especially important for dim-light vision and the periphery, without excluding cone-mediated peripheral vision. [Retinal physiology research](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6153147/).

11. **Medium — The filter experiment is mislabeled as color addition.** [Color and Color Vision, line 111](../contents/ch26ColorAndColorVision.md#L111) asks students to overlay colored sheets or mix crayons under “Exploring Color Addition.” Superimposed filters and pigment mixtures primarily demonstrate subtractive mixing. Use superimposed colored light beams for additive mixing, or relabel and explain this activity. This distinction matters for the preceding RGB display discussion.

12. **Medium — Chirped pulse amplification is not actually explained.** [Vision Correction, line 178](../contents/ch26VisionCorrection.md#L178), repeated in [chapter 30, line 213](../contents/ch30ApplicationOfAtomicExcitationsAndDeexcitations.md#L213), describes passing light over a grating and modifying/chirping the beam. The essential sequence is to stretch a pulse in time, amplify it at reduced peak intensity, then recompress it to obtain high peak power. Include all three stages and distinguish pulse energy from peak power. [Donna Strickland's Nobel lecture](https://www.nobelprize.org/uploads/2018/10/strickland-lecture.pdf).

## Chapter 27 — Wave optics

13. **High — Newton's-ring thickness increments are off by a factor of two.** [Thin Film Interference, line 237](../contents/ch27ThinFilmInterference.md#L237) says successive rings of a given color correspond to one wavelength of additional separation. Adjacent bright rings, or adjacent dark rings, correspond to a thickness change of λ/(2n) at normal incidence, hence λ/2 for an air gap. The round-trip optical path changes by one wavelength. Distinguish film thickness from round-trip optical path.

14. **High — Confocal microscopy is confused with computational removal of blur.** [Microscopy Enhanced by the Wave Characteristics of Light, line 112](../contents/ch27MicroscopyEnhancedByTheWaveCharacteristicsOfLight.md#L112) says out-of-focus regions are subtracted by a computer. Conventional confocal microscopy uses a detection pinhole, or an equivalent confocal aperture arrangement, to reject out-of-focus light. Scanning successive planes provides optical sections for a three-dimensional reconstruction; deconvolution is a separate technique. [Manufacturer's explanation of the confocal principle](https://www.zeiss.com/microscopy/en/products/light-microscopes/confocal-microscopes/lsm-910-for-materials.html).

15. **Medium — An aperture-specific diffraction formula is asserted for every finite beam.** [Limits of Resolution, line 165](../contents/ch27LimitsOfResolution.md#L165) applies θ = 1.22λ/D to any finite-diameter beam, including a laser beam. The factor 1.22 describes the first dark ring for a uniformly illuminated circular aperture. Gaussian-beam divergence has a different coefficient and requires a defined waist radius. Restrict the stated formula to the aperture model and distinguish that model from a typical laser beam.

16. **Medium — Optical activity and stress birefringence are conflated.** [Polarization, line 363](../contents/ch27Polarization.md#L363) calls stressed glass and plastic optically active. The photoelastic effect used in the illustrated stress analysis is stress-induced birefringence: different polarization components acquire different phase delays. It is not generally the rotation of a polarization plane described in the preceding sugar-solution example. Explain the two mechanisms separately.

## Chapter 28 — Special relativity

17. **High — Special relativity is incorrectly restricted to unaccelerated motion.** [Special Relativity, line 25](../contents/ch28SpecialRelativity.md#L25) divides special and general relativity by whether observers accelerate. The restriction is repeated in the chapter's checks and influences [the twin-paradox explanation, line 361](../contents/ch28SimultaneityAndTimeDilation.md#L361). Special relativity handles accelerated worldlines in flat spacetime, including the idealized twin journey. General relativity describes gravitation through spacetime geometry. Explain that changing inertial frames breaks the twins' symmetry; do not imply acceleration alone requires general relativity. [Max Planck Institute explanation of the travelling twins](https://www.einstein-online.info/en/spotlight/twins/).

18. **High — Relative speed is presented as something all observers agree on.** [Length Contraction, line 28](../contents/ch28LengthContraction.md#L28) claims this universally. Two inertial observers agree on the magnitude of their mutual relative velocity; arbitrary third observers do not agree on an object's speed. Qualify the statement before using it in the muon argument. Also correct [the caption at line 69](../contents/ch28LengthContraction.md#L69): in the muon's rest frame, the muon does not travel the contracted path; the atmosphere and Earth move past it.

19. **High — The definition of simultaneity does not distinguish event times from reception times.** [Simultaneity and Time Dilation, line 79](../contents/ch28SimultaneityAndTimeDilation.md#L79) gives receiving light from events as an example of measuring them to occur at the same time. Simultaneous reception establishes simultaneous emission only when the relevant light-travel times are equal. State that event times are assigned using synchronized clocks or corrected signal travel times. The midpoint setup can illustrate this, but the resulting definition needs the qualification.

## Chapter 29 — Quantum physics

20. **High — Energy–time uncertainty is said to permit violations of energy conservation.** [Probability, line 305](../contents/ch29Probability.md#L305) explicitly allows temporary creation of matter from nothing. This is carried into chapter 33's explanation of virtual particles. An uncertainty relation is not permission to violate a conservation law. Present energy–time relations in a stated context, such as lifetime and spectral width; describe virtual particles as intermediate terms in an interaction calculation without a literal energy loan. [Research paper addressing this misconception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.03030).

21. **High — Position–momentum uncertainty is reduced to an uncertain measurement kick.** [Probability, line 73](../contents/ch29Probability.md#L73) and [line 89](../contents/ch29Probability.md#L89) make disturbance the foundation of the principle and identify Δx with the particle's wavelength. The inequality ΔxΔp ≥ ℏ/2 constrains the statistical spreads of a quantum state even before a measurement. A momentum eigenstate can have a definite de Broglie wavelength while being arbitrarily delocalized. Use localized wave packets and a spread of momenta to explain the principle; retain measurement disturbance as an illustration rather than the definition.

22. **High — All UV is treated as ionizing, and visible-light photochemistry is effectively denied.** [Photon Energies and the Electromagnetic Spectrum, line 88](../contents/ch29PhotonEnergiesAndTheElectromagneticSpectrum.md#L88) and [line 203](../contents/ch29PhotonEnergiesAndTheElectromagneticSpectrum.md#L203) imply that a UV photon always has enough energy to ionize atoms and molecules. Much UVA and UVB is classified as non-ionizing; biological damage can result from excitation and photochemical reactions. Ionization thresholds depend on the target. Visible light also drives photochemistry, including the retinal response discussed later in this same section. Distinguish excitation, bond changes, and ionization instead of assigning a universal threshold at the visible/UV boundary.

23. **Medium — Planck is credited with the modern zero-point oscillator formula.** [Quantization of Energy, line 47](../contents/ch29QuantizationOfEnergy.md#L47) attributes E = (n + 1/2)hf to Planck's original blackbody deduction. Present Planck's historical E = nhf quantization and introduce the zero-point term as the later quantum harmonic-oscillator result. Both give energy spacings hf, so the correction does not change the subsequent spacing argument.

## Chapter 30 — Atomic physics

24. **High — Photon energy is said to be directly proportional to wavelength.** [Bohr's Theory of the Hydrogen Atom, line 42](../contents/ch30BohrsTheoryOfTheHydrogenAtom.md#L42) reverses the relation. Replace “wavelengths” with “frequencies,” or say inversely proportional to wavelength: E = hf = hc/λ.

25. **High — Thermoluminescence dating is explained backward.** [Applications of Atomic Excitations and De-Excitations, line 133](../contents/ch30ApplicationOfAtomicExcitationsAndDeexcitations.md#L133) and its caption at line 139 say firing stores excited states that gradually decay, so less light means an older ceramic. Firing largely resets the traps. Natural irradiation subsequently repopulates them, and the accumulated signal, together with the environmental dose rate, supplies the age. Rewrite both the paragraph and caption. [Oxford account of the dating method](https://www.physics.ox.ac.uk/sites/default/files/news_files/history-of-clarendon-laboratory.pdf).

26. **High — The text contradicts itself about electrons inside the nucleus.** [The Wave Nature of Matter Causes Quantization, line 77](../contents/ch30TheWaveNatureOfMatterCausesQuantization.md#L77) says an electron cannot exist closer than the lowest orbit or inside the nucleus. [Quantum Numbers and Rules, line 53](../contents/ch30QuantumNumbersAndRules.md#L53) correctly describes nonzero nuclear overlap. Explain that a bound atomic wavefunction can overlap the nucleus; atomic stability does not impose a hard exclusion radius at the Bohr radius. Keep the circular standing-wave argument explicitly labeled as a semiclassical model.

27. **High — The quantum-number recap loses s states and gives hydrogen an impossible ground-state label.** [Quantum Numbers and Rules, line 251](../contents/ch30QuantumNumbersAndRules.md#L251) lists l starting at 1, whereas the earlier equation and table correctly start at 0. [The caption at line 311](../contents/ch30QuantumNumbersAndRules.md#L311) labels the ground state (0, 0, 0), despite n starting at 1. Correct these to l = 0, …, n−1 and (n, l, m_l) = (1, 0, 0).

28. **High — Common laser diodes are incorrectly said to be made of silicon.** [Applications of Atomic Excitations and De-Excitations, line 234](../contents/ch30ApplicationOfAtomicExcitationsAndDeexcitations.md#L234) assigns ordinary pointer/diode lasers to silicon. Conventional devices use suitable direct-band-gap compound semiconductors, such as GaAs-related materials for many red/infrared devices and GaN-related materials for blue devices. Silicon's indirect band gap makes the blanket description misleading.

29. **High — Laser-driven fusion compression is attributed to photon momentum.** [Applications of Atomic Excitations and De-Excitations, line 282](../contents/ch30ApplicationOfAtomicExcitationsAndDeexcitations.md#L282) says the impinging photons' momentum causes the implosion. In conventional inertial-confinement fusion, ablation of the capsule produces a rocket-like inward compression. At NIF the lasers first generate X-rays in a hohlraum. Chapter 32's ablation-based account is closer to the correct mechanism. [LLNL target-design explanation](https://lasers.llnl.gov/news/designing-for-ignition-precise-changes-yield-historic-results).

30. **Medium — Population inversion is defined too narrowly, and a metastable state is made universal.** [Applications of Atomic Excitations and De-Excitations, line 155](../contents/ch30ApplicationOfAtomicExcitationsAndDeexcitations.md#L155) requires a majority of all atoms in a metastable state, then [line 218](../contents/ch30ApplicationOfAtomicExcitationsAndDeexcitations.md#L218) asserts that all lasers rely on a metastable state or phosphorescent material. For the illustrated three-level laser, a majority requirement can apply. In general, inversion concerns the upper and lower lasing levels, with degeneracy considered; four-level and semiconductor systems need a broader account. Present the pictured system as one example, not the universal mechanism.

## Chapter 31 — Radioactivity and nuclear physics

31. **High — Carbon-14 production is assigned to solar neutrinos.** [Half-Life and Activity, line 76](../contents/ch31HalfLifeAndActivity.md#L76) should instead describe cosmic-ray-produced secondary neutrons reacting with atmospheric nitrogen: ¹⁴N + n → ¹⁴C + p. [NOAA explanation](https://www.gml.noaa.gov/outreach/isotopes/decay.html).

32. **High — Proton-rich beta decay has the wrong sign.** [Nuclear Decay and Conservation Laws, line 429](../contents/ch31NuclearDecayAndConservationLaws.md#L429) says proton-rich nuclides undergo β− decay or electron capture to reduce their proton number. It should say β+ decay or electron capture. The incorrect sign contradicts the decay equations immediately above and would lead directly to wrong daughter nuclei.

33. **High — Lepton-flavor conservation and massless neutrinos are asserted without their limits.** [Nuclear Decay and Conservation Laws, line 231](../contents/ch31NuclearDecayAndConservationLaws.md#L231) says electron-family number is conserved in all circumstances, while [line 281](../contents/ch31NuclearDecayAndConservationLaws.md#L281) calls the emitted antineutrino massless. Flavor bookkeeping is appropriate for these elementary beta-decay reactions, but neutrino oscillations change individual flavor populations; at least two neutrino mass eigenstates have nonzero mass. Label negligible mass as an approximation and qualify the flavor statement. Coordinate this correction with chapters 33–34. [Nobel explanation of the evidence](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/2015/press-release).

34. **High — The weak interaction is incorrectly included in the force binding nuclei together.** [Substructure of the Nucleus, line 265](../contents/ch31SubstructureOfTheNucleus.md#L265) says weak and strong forces both contribute to nuclear stability like short-range glue. The residual strong interaction binds nucleons; the weak interaction enables transformations such as beta decay. Distinguish binding from the decay processes that change a nucleus's composition. [CERN explanation of residual strong binding](https://wwwcompass.cern.ch/compass/wwwsmc/target/fundamentalparticles.html).

## Chapter 32 — Applications of nuclear physics

35. **High — Diagnostic radiation doses mix incompatible quantities and include a seriously misleading dental number.** [Biological Effects of Ionizing Radiation, line 639](../contents/ch32BiologicalEffectsAndIonizingRadiation.md#L639) quotes about 5 mSv per dental X-ray and describes the listed doses as applying only to the imaged tissue. Specify whether each number is absorbed organ dose, equivalent organ dose, or effective dose. For a typical intraoral dental image, the FDA lists an adult effective dose of approximately 0.005–0.01 mSv; the current unqualified 5-mSv figure is hundreds to a thousand times that range. It also contradicts the passage's ranking of chest X-rays as the lowest-dose study. [FDA dose comparison](https://www.fda.gov/radiation-emitting-products/initiative-reduce-unnecessary-radiation-exposure-medical-imaging/white-paper-initiative-reduce-unnecessary-radiation-exposure-medical-imaging).

36. **High — Absorbed dose, radiation protection weighting, and biological effectiveness are conflated.** [Biological Effects of Ionizing Radiation, line 104](../contents/ch32BiologicalEffectsAndIonizingRadiation.md#L104) treats RBE and quality factor as interchangeable and presents Sv = Gy × RBE as a universal definition. [The procedure at line 548](../contents/ch32BiologicalEffectsAndIonizingRadiation.md#L548) then directs the reader to convert deposited energy per mass straight to Sv before considering weighting. Teach absorbed dose D in Gy first, equivalent dose using radiation weighting factors, and effective dose using tissue weighting factors. Discuss RBE separately because it depends on the biological endpoint and irradiation conditions. Multiplying by a dimensionless weighting factor is not a fixed unit conversion. [IAEA definitions](https://nucleus.iaea.org/sites/nss-oui/Published%20Collections/m_3761d926-c16f-4477-a63b-741a9db1c16c/m_3761d926-c16f-4477-a63b-741a9db1c16c__50_0.Html).

37. **High — Therapy doses are given in Sv/rem, and curative lung radiotherapy is dismissed.** [Therapeutic Uses of Ionizing Radiation, line 81](../contents/ch32TherapeuticUsesOfIonizingRadiation.md#L81) and the adjacent table should use absorbed dose in Gy, or a clearly specified RBE-weighted absorbed dose where appropriate. Sv is a radiation-protection quantity. The same paragraph says lung cancer cannot ordinarily be cured by radiation and describes mainly palliation. Explain that intent depends on stage and tumor type; definitive radiotherapy, including SBRT for appropriate early-stage non-small-cell lung cancers, can be curative. [ICRP guidance on dose quantities](https://www.icrp.org/publication.asp?id=ICRP+Publication+118), [NCI treatment evidence](https://www.cancer.gov/types/lung/hp/non-small-cell-lung-treatment-pdq).

38. **High — A fusion-related reaction is duplicated with incompatible Q values.** [Fusion, line 178](../contents/ch32Fusion.md#L178) gives n + ¹H → ²H + γ with 20.68 MeV, then [line 184](../contents/ch32Fusion.md#L184) gives the identical reaction with 2.22 MeV. The latter agrees with deuteron binding. Correct or remove the first equation after checking which reaction was intended; changing only its numerical label would leave an unexplained duplicate.

39. **High — Fusion milestones are obsolete and energy-gain measures are insufficiently distinguished.** [Fusion, line 190](../contents/ch32Fusion.md#L190) says laboratory ignition has not been achieved and break-even has nearly been reached. NIF achieved ignition and target gain above one on December 5, 2022, producing 3.15 MJ of fusion energy from 2.05 MJ of laser energy delivered to the target. This is not net electrical gain for the facility. Distinguish target gain, plasma/heating gain, and whole-facility energy balance. The ITER discussion and caption still predict completion in 2018; replace that forecast with a dated statement tied to ITER's revised plan. [LLNL experimental account](https://www.llnl.gov/article/50801/llnls-breakthrough-ignition-experiment-highlighted-physical-review-letters), [ITER's revised baseline](https://www.iter.org/node/20687/new-baseline-prioritize-robust-start-exploitation).

40. **High — Food irradiation is described through unsupported residual-hazard speculation.** [Food Irradiation, line 68](../contents/ch32FoodIrradiation.md#L68) suggests potentially carcinogenic radiolytic products may yet cause long-term effects once consumption becomes widespread. Rework this around the safety assessment of approved processes and conditions, consistent with the correction to chapter 24. Also correct [the definition of free radicals at line 69](../contents/ch32FoodIrradiation.md#L69): an unpaired electron is the defining feature; oxygen or hydrogen content is not. [FDA assessment and explanation](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/food-irradiation-what-you-need-know).

41. **Medium — Solar fusion is incorrectly moved to a helium-core boundary.** [Fusion's caption at line 120](../contents/ch32Fusion.md#L120) puts the Sun's fusion primarily at the boundary of its helium core. The main-sequence Sun burns hydrogen throughout its central fusion region; hydrogen-shell burning around an inert helium core characterizes a later evolutionary stage. Align the caption with the preceding central-fusion explanation. The following text also associates the three neutrino flavors with different nuclear-decay types; identify them by electron, muon, and tau weak-interaction associations instead.

## Chapter 33 — Particle physics

42. **High — Established electroweak theory is confused with a verified grand unified theory.** [GUTs, line 17](../contents/ch33GUTS.md#L17) describes a GUT as linking all four forces; [the box at line 121](../contents/ch33GUTS.md#L121) then treats GUT as experimentally successful for all four. Distinguish the tested Standard Model/electroweak framework, hypothetical grand unification of strong and electroweak interactions, and a further theory including quantum gravity. The present wording makes speculative unification sound established.

43. **High — The Higgs discovery is still a possible future event in 2012.** [GUTs, line 143](../contents/ch33GUTS.md#L143) says direct discovery may occur during 2012. Replace this with the ATLAS/CMS discovery and a clear explanation of the Higgs field and electroweak symmetry breaking. Also remove the suggestion that simply giving a W or Z more kinetic energy transforms it into a massless particle. [CERN Higgs account](https://home.cern/science/physics/higgs-boson).

44. **High — Virtual-particle exchange repeats the energy-conservation misconception.** [The Yukawa Particle, line 30](../contents/ch33TheYukawaParticleAndTheHeisenbergUncertaintyPrincipleRevisited.md#L30) and its caption say a pion is created by a permitted temporary violation of mass–energy conservation. Rewrite together with chapter 29. A useful introductory replacement relates the force range to a mediator's mass scale without presenting the uncertainty relation as permission to create energy. The discussion should also distinguish real pions from virtual exchanged pions. [Research discussion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.03030).

45. **High — Jet counting is falsely treated as a universal count of a proton's three quarks.** [Quarks, line 545](../contents/ch33Quarks.md#L545) says momentum conservation requires three jets and that only three appear. Jet multiplicity depends on the hard process, gluon radiation, and event selection; it is not fixed at three by a proton's valence-quark content. Explain deep-inelastic scattering and jets as complementary evidence without this false counting rule.

46. **High — Gauge bosons are assigned three generations analogous to quarks and leptons.** [Quarks, line 639](../contents/ch33Quarks.md#L639) and Figure 6 present this as a particle classification, with a later qualification that its meaning is uncertain. This is not the Standard Model's generational structure. Replace the diagram/classification with three generations of fermions, the gauge bosons, and the Higgs scalar. Keep unsupported visual analogies out of the factual summary.

47. **Medium — The gluon explanation misstates both the force and color structure.** [GUTs, line 69](../contents/ch33GUTS.md#L69) contains a sentence apparently describing gluons as carriers of weak/electromagnetic forces, and labels two of the eight gluons as carrying no color. State that gluons mediate the strong interaction and form a color octet. The two diagonal color combinations are not color-singlet particles analogous to the photon; the proposed singlet is absent from QCD. Correct the accompanying caption/diagram as well. [CERN gluon definition](https://opendata.cern.ch/glossary/Gluon).

## Chapter 34 — Frontiers of physics

48. **High — Cosmological distance is equated with light-travel time at arbitrary distances.** [Cosmology and Particle Physics, line 72](../contents/ch34CosmologyAndParticlePhysics.md#L72) says the time in years is the same as distance in light years, then applies this to a galaxy whose light supposedly left 14 billion years ago. In an expanding universe, present distance, luminosity distance, and lookback time differ. Introduce the distinction and use a clearly defined distance measure. Restrict the elementary travel-time approximation to sufficiently nearby objects; remove the unsupported record-holder claim.

49. **High — The Big Bang is repeatedly described as a localized explosion, and cosmic ages conflict.** [Cosmology and Particle Physics, line 89](../contents/ch34CosmologyAndParticlePhysics.md#L89) and captions at lines 158 and 184 describe matter expelled by an explosion. Explain expansion of space from an early hot, dense state, with no center within the universe. Use a consistent approximate age of 13.8 billion years; the captions' 10–20-billion-year range and the 14-billion-year lookback statement need revision. [NASA explanation of cosmological redshift](https://science.nasa.gov/asset/webb/what-is-cosmological-redshift/), [NASA cosmology glossary](https://science.nasa.gov/universe/glossary/).

50. **High — Hubble's constant has the wrong units, and its example has an arithmetic inconsistency.** [Cosmology and Particle Physics, line 123](../contents/ch34CosmologyAndParticlePhysics.md#L123) writes 20 km/s · Mly. It should be 20 (km/s)/Mly. The example introduces 100,000 km/s but substitutes 10,000 km/s while reporting 5,000 Mly. Use consistent input values. Also explain that cz ≈ H₀d is a nearby-universe approximation; doubling distance does not generally double observed redshift at large z.

51. **High — Known CP violation is presented as the explanation of cosmic matter dominance.** [Cosmology and Particle Physics, line 213](../contents/ch34CosmologyAndParticlePhysics.md#L213) claims the asymmetry in neutral-kaon decay produced the required matter excess. CP violation is relevant, but the known Standard Model sources are insufficient to explain the observed imbalance, and kaon decay does not by itself establish baryogenesis. Present the origin of the cosmic asymmetry as an open problem. [CERN account of the unresolved asymmetry](https://home.cern/new-piece-matter-antimatter-puzzle/).

52. **High — Spatial curvature and the universe's fate are treated as the same question.** [Dark Matter and Closure, line 53](../contents/ch34DarkMatterAndClosure.md#L53) identifies open/closed with expanding forever/recollapsing, and [line 97](../contents/ch34DarkMatterAndClosure.md#L97) says discovering a cosmological constant establishes flatness and eternal expansion. Those equivalences require restricted cosmological models. Separate total density/spatial curvature from expansion dynamics and the properties of dark energy. For a standard ΛCDM account, introduce roughly 5% ordinary matter, 27% dark matter, and 68% dark energy with the model and measurement date identified. [ESA/Planck inventory](https://www.esa.int/ESA_Multimedia/Images/2013/03/Planck_cosmic_recipe).

53. **High — Neutrino mass is treated as unresolved, and ordinary neutrinos are promoted as the main dark matter.** [Dark Matter and Closure, line 152](../contents/ch34DarkMatterAndClosure.md#L152) calls neutrinos thought massless and later says oscillations may explain solar-neutrino deficits. Oscillations and nonzero neutrino masses are established. Standard relic active neutrinos cannot supply most of the dark matter consistent with their masses and structure formation. Distinguish that conclusion from hypotheses involving sterile neutrinos or nonstandard production. This section contradicts chapter 32's account of the resolved solar-neutrino problem. [Nobel evidence](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/2015/press-release), [particle-physics review of the dark-matter constraints](https://lss.fnal.gov/archive/2022/conf/fermilab-conf-22-184-t-v.pdf).

54. **High — Dark-matter candidates are assigned incorrect particle categories.** [Dark Matter and Closure, line 189](../contents/ch34DarkMatterAndClosure.md#L189) calls WIMPs previously unknown leptons and treats axions as another WIMP category that only feels the weak force. WIMPs are a broad hypothetical class, not necessarily leptons; neutralinos are one proposed example. Axions are a distinct class of light bosonic candidates, not conventional WIMPs, and their proposed interactions are not characterized simply by the weak force. Rewrite this taxonomy before adding updated experimental status. [Research review of WIMP and axion candidates](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9710467).

55. **High — The neutron-star mass limit is much too high.** [General Relativity and Quantum Gravity, line 150](../contents/ch34GeneralRelativityAndQuantumTheory.md#L150) says a neutron star can remain stable up to approximately eight solar masses. An introductory limit of order two to three solar masses, with dependence on the equation of state and rotation, is appropriate. Eight solar masses may be confused with a progenitor-star scale; it is not the compact remnant's supported mass. Also explain that pressure support involves degeneracy and nuclear interactions rather than just “resistance by the strong force.” [NASA discussion of neutron-star constraints](https://heasarc.gsfc.nasa.gov/docs/nicer/science_nuggets/20250605.html).

56. **Medium — The equivalence principle is stated globally instead of locally.** [General Relativity and Quantum Gravity, line 42](../contents/ch34GeneralRelativityAndQuantumTheory.md#L42) says acceleration and gravity are indistinguishable “in all situations.” The elevator argument applies locally; tidal effects reveal spacetime curvature over an extended region. Add the local qualification so the analogy does not erase the physical content of curved spacetime.

57. **Medium — Speculative quantum-gravity claims are presented with unsupported precision.** [General Relativity and Quantum Gravity, line 260](../contents/ch34GeneralRelativityAndQuantumTheory.md#L260) gives a possible minimum time of 10⁻⁹⁵ s and treats force unification at 10⁻⁴³ s as established. [Superstrings, line 20](../contents/ch34Superstrings.md#L20) equates independent quantum numbers with separate spacetime dimensions. Remove the unexplained numerical minimum; distinguish Planck scales, hypotheses about spacetime discreteness, extra dimensions, and actual predictions of particular models. Quantum gravity is not automatically a complete unification of all interactions. These sections need a conceptual rewrite with named theoretical assumptions and references.
